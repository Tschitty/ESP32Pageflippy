Controller search: …

The search runs for 60 seconds after start-up, after “Pair new controller” or after pressing the BOOT button on the ESP32. Wi-Fi is slower during the search. A paired DualSense reconnects without a search, a Fire TV Remote only during a search.

Controller mapping

InputNextBack

Connect a wire, aluminium foil or copper tape to GPIO32 and GPIO33. Current reading: – (touching makes the value drop clearly). Leave it off if nothing is connected.

Assign on the controller: hold D-pad up and press a button = that button turns to the next page (right side vibrates 2x). Hold D-pad down and press a button = previous page (left side vibrates 2x). Hold R1 + L1 + Cross for 5 seconds = default mapping (both vibrate 3x).
